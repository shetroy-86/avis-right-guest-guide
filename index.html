A note from us 🌿

The bedrooms are upstairs, and on warm days the whole-house AC can have a harder time keeping them cool. We've added supplemental AC units in both bedrooms for your comfort. When you leave, we'd so appreciate you turning the whole-house thermostat up a few degrees — it helps bring things back to a comfortable temperature quickly for the next guests.

Welcome In

A few things to know when you first arrive.

Electronic Keypad Locks

lightbulb

It provides less resistance and helps the batteries last longer if you pull the door closed whenever the deadbolt is moving electronically.

To Unlock

Enter your code, then press the ✓ checkmark. Pull the door toward until you hear the lock stop moving.

To Lock

Press the ✓ checkmark. Again, please pull the door shut while it operates.

battery_low

If the ✓ turns red or orange, the battery is low. We will happily stop by to replace them, just send a text to Sheena. If you would rather replace them yourself, spare batteries are in the toolbox (in the lower kitchen cabinet beside the refrigerator).

Bedroom Air Conditioners

We install these because the upstairs bedrooms can be hard to keep cool on hot days; they make a real difference.

They also mean the house bounces back much faster after warming up.

So when you head out for a few hours, turning the temperature up a few degrees to help conserve energy is genuinely appreciated. 🌿

Large Bedroom

Portable Floor Unit

Please try not to disturb the position of this unit, or the vent and hose. The hot air vent and drain lines are routed out of the house via the attic.

Small Bedroom

Window Unit

We only install this unit if there is someone occupying the small bedroom. If you would like to have it installed, please let us know.

Weekly Schedule

delete_outline Pick-up

General Trash

Trash is collected Friday morning. Since the truck sometimes comes early, it is best to roll the tote out to the street Thursday evening.

If there is a holiday, or the truck misses a Friday pickup due to a breakdown or staffing issue, they will come by on Saturday to empty your tote.

Where to Find the Trash Bin

The trash tote is on the sidewalk to the right of the garage. When rolling it out to the street, position the tote with the silver bar facing the street. Roll it back after Friday morning pickup.
